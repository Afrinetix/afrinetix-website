FAQ

Questions We Get Asked Every Day

How much does a website cost?+
Our websites start from KES 15,000 for an SME Starter site, KES 35,000 for a Business Growth site, and KES 65,000 for an E-Commerce store. Custom web applications start from KES 120,000. Every project gets a detailed quote after a free discovery call.
How long does development take?+
Starter websites typically launch in 1-2 weeks. Business Growth sites take 2-4 weeks. E-Commerce stores take 3-5 weeks. Custom web applications are scoped individually, but we always provide a clear timeline before starting.
Do packages include hosting?+
Hosting is not included in the one-time build fee, but we can set it up for you. We recommend reliable Kenyan and international hosts (typically KES 5,000-15,000/year depending on your needs). Our maintenance packages include ongoing hosting support.
Can you integrate M-Pesa into the website?+
Yes. We integrate M-Pesa STK Push, Lipa Na M-Pesa, and Daraja API into websites, e-commerce stores, and custom web applications. M-Pesa integration is included in E-Commerce packages and available as an add-on for other packages.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?+
Yes. We offer three maintenance plans starting from KES 1,500/month covering security, backups, updates, and support. All new websites come with 30 days of free post-launch support before transitioning to a maintenance plan.
Can you redesign my existing website?+
Absolutely. Website redesigns are one of our most common projects. We audit your current site, identify what is working and what is not, and rebuild it with better design, faster performance, and stronger conversion focus. Pricing follows our standard Web Development packages.
